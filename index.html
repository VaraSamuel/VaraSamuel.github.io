Other Research & Independent Work

Queuesight↗

Quantitative Research, Market Microstructure

A limit order book forecasting system evaluated the way a trading desk would: after crossing the spread, with latency, and with a statistical guarantee on how often the trades it takes lose money. A queue-reactive simulator with a hidden informed-trader regime generates the market data; three models (logistic regression on order flow imbalance, gradient-boosted trees on microstructure features, and a DeepLOB-style conv and GRU network) forecast direction; and two distribution-free methods, Learn-then-Test risk control and adaptive conformal prediction with delayed labels, decide when a forecast is worth trading on. Across eight independently simulated markets, the Learn-then-Test gate certified its loss-rate bound on 8 of 8 seeds and produced the highest net PnL per trade of any strategy tested, including an always-trade baseline and a tuned confidence threshold.

Stack: Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Streamlit, pytest.
